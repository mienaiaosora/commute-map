Mean travel time to work, US metro areas, 2019

One-way minutes, workers 16+ who did not work at home (ACS 2019 1-year, table R0801). Circle area is proportional to 2019 population; 384 metropolitan statistical areas. US mean: 27.6 minutes.

Sources: US Census Bureau, ACS 2019 1-year geographic comparison tables (R0801 commute time, R0804 transit share); Census population estimates (Vintage 2019); 2019 CBSA Gazetteer for locations. Only metros above about 65,000 people appear in the ACS 1-year file. Alaska and Hawaii metros are shown in inset positions.
