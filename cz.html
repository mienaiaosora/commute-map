Mean travel time to work by commuting zone, 2015–2019

One-way minutes, workers 16+ who did not work at home. 583 commuting zones (2020 definitions), each a group of counties that forms one local labor market. Each zone's mean is its total commute minutes divided by its total commuters. US mean: 26.9 minutes.

Sources: US Census Bureau, American Community Survey 2015–2019 5-year estimates (tables B08012, B08013, B08301, B01003), aggregated from counties; 2020 commuting zones from Fowler (2024), Scientific Data, built from ACS 2016–2020 county-to-county commuting flows; 2019 Census cartographic county boundaries (1:20m). Zones are named after their most populous county. Commute data are by place of residence.
